Our Story

A Cosy Corner of Thailand on the High Street

Royal Thai is Stony Stratford's relaxed neighbourhood Thai — Asian decor, generous portions and a menu of popular Thai dishes cooked to order, from Thai tapas and lunchtime sets to rich curries and seafood.

  • Authentic Thai kitchen — curries, stir-fries, noodles & Thai tapas
  • Dine-in, takeaway & delivery via Deliveroo and Uber Eats
  • Live music nights, full bar and table service
  • Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options — everyone welcome
